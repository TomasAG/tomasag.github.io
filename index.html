I'm a PhD candidate in Communication at Stanford University, where I'm a Knight-Hennessy Scholar. I research technology governance from a sociocultural perspective.

I graduated from MIT's Comparative Media Studies program in 2022. Before MIT, I earned my B.A. in Social Sciences from Universidad Torcuato Di Tella and worked in the President of Argentina's Office.

My dissertation is an ethnographic study of how people who work to make AI safer think about agency: both their own and that of the systems they are trying to shape.
